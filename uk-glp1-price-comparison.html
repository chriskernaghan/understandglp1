Programme or pharmacy: which should you choose?

The providers above fall into two camps, and the right one depends on what you want beyond the pen itself.

Online pharmacies (Pharmacy2U, Ashcroft, Boots Online Doctor and similar) sell you the medication with a clinical consultation attached. They're usually the cheapest route, the process is transactional, and you're free to switch to whoever is cheapest next month. What you don't get is structured support — no coaching, no app, no community.

Weight-loss programmes (Numan, Juniper, Voy, Second Nature and similar) wrap the medication in a subscription: health coaching, progress tracking, dietitian content, sometimes anti-sickness medication included. They cost more — often substantially more at higher doses — and the headline price is typically an intro offer for month one. For some people the accountability is genuinely worth it; for others it's an expensive app they stop opening by week three.

A pattern worth noticing in the table: programmes are aggressive on starting-dose pricing and expensive at the top end, while pharmacies are steadier across the range. If you expect to titrate up to higher doses, compare the right-hand column, not the headline.

The discount code small print

Almost every discount in this table is a first-order offer. Your second month reverts to the standard price, so the question that matters isn't "what's the cheapest first month?" but "what's the cheapest ongoing price from a provider I trust?" One quirk worth knowing: Voy's discount is delivered by email after you sign up to their mailing list — there's no public code to copy, which is why their entry says so.

Buy safely: Only ever buy GLP-1 medication from a pharmacy registered with the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC). You can check any pharmacy's registration free at pharmacyregulation.org. The MHRA has repeatedly warned about counterfeit weight-loss injections sold through social media and unregistered websites — a price that looks too good to be true is the biggest red flag there is.

Switching providers is easier than you'd think

If you're already on treatment and your provider has crept up in price, you are not locked in. Most providers will accept a switch if you can show evidence of your current prescription — typically a photo of your medication box or a screenshot of a recent order. You'll still complete their consultation, and some providers apply minimum BMI rules to switchers, but the process usually takes days, not weeks. Cancel any subscription with your old provider before your next billing date.
