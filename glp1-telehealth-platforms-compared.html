GLP-1 Telehealth Platforms Compared — US & UK

Updated March 2026·14 min read
TL;DR
  • Telehealth platforms let you get a GLP-1 prescription and ongoing support entirely online — no GP or in-person visit required.
  • US platforms vary widely in cost: expect $200–$500/month all-in depending on medication and service tier.
  • UK platforms are generally cheaper due to NHS pricing context, but medication availability is more limited.
  • App quality and clinical support differ significantly — some platforms are check-the-box, others offer genuine coaching.
  • None of these platforms are inherently better or worse — the right choice depends on your budget, medication preference, and how much support you want.

Getting a GLP-1 prescription no longer means waiting weeks for a specialist appointment. Telehealth platforms have made it possible to be evaluated, prescribed, and supported entirely online — often within 24–48 hours. But with dozens of services now operating across the US and UK, the differences between them matter.

This comparison focuses on two things that affect your experience most: what you'll actually pay and what happens after you start. We've left out platforms that only sell compounded semaglutide without proper clinical oversight, and we haven't accepted payment from any service listed here.

A note on pricing: Telehealth pricing changes frequently. Figures in this article reflect publicly available pricing as of March 2026 and should be verified directly with each platform before you sign up. Medication costs are separate from consultation fees unless otherwise stated.

🇺🇸 US Platforms

The US market is the most developed and the most competitive. Several well-funded platforms now offer end-to-end GLP-1 care — evaluation, prescription, medication delivery, and ongoing check-ins — all through a single app or website. Pricing is almost entirely out-of-pocket for most patients, since insurance coverage for weight management remains inconsistent.

🇬🇧 UK Platforms

The UK market is smaller and more tightly regulated — all prescriptions require a UK-registered prescriber, and the range of available medications is narrower than in the US. Wegovy (semaglutide) and Mounjaro (tirzepatide) are the primary options available through private telehealth; compounded GLP-1 medications are not legally available in the UK in the same way as the US.

Costs are generally lower than the US, but medication supply has historically been patchy — particularly for Wegovy, which faced significant shortages through 2024 and into 2025.

What to Actually Look For When Choosing

With all the options above, here's a simple framework for narrowing it down. The right platform depends on three things:

1. How much support do you want?

If you've done your research, understand the medication, and primarily need a prescriber and reliable delivery — a lower-cost, more basic platform (Manual in the UK; Hims & Hers in the US) is a reasonable fit. If you want structured help building habits, managing side effects, and staying on track, the platforms with dedicated coaching (Noom Med, Juniper) are worth the higher cost.

2. Does medication type matter to you?

In the US, the availability of compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide at lower price points is a genuine advantage — but it comes with caveats. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and quality can vary between compounding pharmacies. If brand-name is important to you (or if you have insurance that may cover it), WeightWatchers Clinic is the clearest pathway. In the UK, this question doesn't arise — all platforms prescribe brand-name medications only.

3. What happens if things go wrong?

Side effects, stalled progress, and dose adjustments are all normal parts of the GLP-1 journey. Before you sign up, check: How quickly can you reach a clinician? Is it asynchronous messaging only, or can you get a real conversation? What's the cancellation and refund policy? Platforms with slower response times and rigid subscription terms can become frustrating when you need flexibility.

Worth knowing: Several of these platforms offer first-month discounts or free consultations. These promotions change regularly — it's worth checking their sites directly before committing, and reading recent user reviews on independent forums like Reddit's r/Ozempic and r/Mounjaro for unfiltered experiences.

The Bottom Line

Telehealth has genuinely democratised access to GLP-1 medications — the platforms above are all operating legally, with qualified prescribers, and most offer a meaningfully better experience than navigating the traditional healthcare system for weight management.

The honest advice is this: don't optimise only for price. The cheapest option per month can become the most expensive if poor support leads to stopping medication early, or if side effects go unmanaged. At the same time, the most expensive platform isn't automatically the best — particularly for experienced patients who know what they need.

Take the time to read the fine print on cancellation, talk to the support team before you subscribe, and look at independent reviews. The right platform is one you'll actually stay with.
